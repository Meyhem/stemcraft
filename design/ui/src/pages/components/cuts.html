Cut markers & cut list

A cut is a split point stored as an integer sample index at 48 kHz (D-03). Cut N ends track N and starts track N+1, so the waveform and the table are two views of the same list: moving a cut in either moves it in both. Detected silences are only proposals (D8-04) — applied when you press Use proposed boundaries, and from then on indistinguishable from cuts you placed.

The waveform is the whole album master, played from a plain <audio> element; the part already played is drawn in the accent, the rest in light grey. It zooms and pans exactly like the Song view (wheel at the pointer, drag across it to zoom to a range, Shift+wheel or the scrollbar to pan), down to 100 px/s — the album peaks hold 10 buckets/s (D8-12), so closer shows nothing new. Past that, exact placement is what the millisecond fields are for.

Press on a cut's number, dragMoves exactly that cut, even where cuts overlap on screen.
Cuts overlapping on screenThe drag direction picks the one that can move (rightmost dragging right, leftmost dragging left).

Start and End are typed as m:ss.mmm (or h:mm:ss.mmm, or bare seconds). Track 1's start and the last track's end are the album's edges, not cuts, so they are plain text. A time that is not a time, or that would cross a neighbouring cut, is refused with the allowed range and the cut does not move — never clamped to something you did not type (N-08). Escape abandons the edit. ▶ plays from the track's first sample; × removes the cut after the track, merging it with the next (its title survives). Filenames are shown before the split runs, exactly as it will write them.
