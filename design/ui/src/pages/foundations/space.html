Hit targets — two tiers

This is where the music-stand constraint bites hardest. Controls split into performance controls, hit imprecisely while holding an instrument, and setup controls, touched with both hands free. A control's tier is a design decision recorded in the component, not a size picked by eye.

Motion

120ms state flips · 200ms panels and modals · 400ms progress bars. Easing cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1).

Hard rule: the playhead and waveform cursor are driven by requestAnimationFrame off the audio engine's clock, never by a CSS transition. D-07 makes every waveform a slave to that clock; a CSS-animated playhead would be a second clock telling a different, smoother lie. prefers-reduced-motion zeroes every duration above and changes nothing about the playhead.
