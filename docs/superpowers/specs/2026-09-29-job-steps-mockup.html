Every job, step by step

Each job kind declares its steps in stemcraft_lib. The steps are written when the job is queued, and the worker advances them while it runs. The websocket already pushes every change to a job row, so steps update live with no new transport. One shared StepList component draws them in the Job queue and in the Add-song modal.

1 · Job queue: live section

One row per job, as it is now. Each row has a disclosure arrow. The running job opens by itself, and the rest stay collapsed with a thin segment strip showing where each one is. Each step shows its own duration, and the running step shows its live percentage. A failed job's traceback now appears under the step that failed, instead of in a separate banner list.

3 · Add-song modal, now built from job steps

The modal no longer works out its steps from which files exist. It shows the import job and then the song's separate and analyze jobs, each as a small group drawn with the same StepList. Separate and analyze are queued by the worker after import finishes, so until they exist their steps come from the declared list and are drawn pending.

Step marks: one vocabulary everywhere

  • ✓ done · ● running with a live percentage and bar · ○ pending · ! failed with the real error inline · dashed – skipped with a reason · ■ cancelled with where it stopped.
  • The optional detail text (for example "segment 11 of 17", "4 of 11", "bass.opus") comes from the worker as it runs and is never guessed by the UI.
  • The collapsed row's segment strip is the same data drawn small, so you can see where a job is without opening it.
